**OPENING VIDEO SEQUENCE**

Production Script

*\"The Fairy Tale Book\"*

**Duration:** 55--70 seconds

**Format:** Pre-produced video asset (plays once, not skippable on first viewing)

**Visual Style:** Illustrated watercolor paintings with gentle parallax animation --- a living storybook. NOT 3D. NOT anime. Think Arthur Rackham meets Ori and the Blind Forest: luminous, hand-painted, layered with soft light and movement. Every page should feel like a painting that breathes.

**Art Production:** Midjourney for illustrated frames → Photopea for parallax layer separation (2--3 layers per page) → AI video tools (Runway Gen-4 preferred) for gentle motion

**Audio Production:** ElevenLabs narrator voice --- warm, older, grandparent-like. This is NOT the Guide Bird. This is a storybook being read aloud.

**Music:** Original score --- begins with a single instrument (harp or music box), grows through Pages 1--3, turns dark at Page 4, fades to near-silence at Page 5, stays sparse through Page 6, resolves into warmth at the Final Page.

**TTS / Personalization Note:** The Opening Storybook contains NO personalization variables — no `{childName}`, no `{therapistName}`. The narrator (Myrrhin voice — same ElevenLabs voice profile used for all Phase B meditations) speaks to no one; this is a storybook being read aloud. This video CAN be pre-produced as a single baked asset (visual + audio together) for maximum polish and zero latency on the child's very first experience. Alternatively, Myrrhin's audio can be TTS-rendered once at build time and baked into the video — either approach works since no per-child variation is needed. See `TTS_PERSONALIZATION_PIPELINE_v1.md` §2.3 and §5.2 for the full decision context.

# THE BOOK ITSELF

Before any page opens, the screen is dark.

A soft golden light blooms in the center of the darkness --- warm, intimate, like a candle being lit in a quiet room. The light reveals the surface of a very old book lying on a surface we never fully see. Dark wood, maybe. Or stone. We don't linger on it.

**The book** is leather-bound, thick, heavy with age. Its cover is embossed with Celtic knotwork --- interlacing patterns that seem to shift subtly in the candlelight. The leather is cracked and worn at the edges. A faint golden shimmer moves through the knotwork lines, tracing the patterns like slow-moving liquid light. At the center of the cover, barely visible beneath centuries of wear, is a circular emblem --- six small stones arranged around a tree. The stones are dark. Dormant.

The book trembles. Just once. Then it opens by itself --- slowly, heavily --- and the first page is revealed.

***Production Note --- The Book Opening:** This 5--7 second pre-page sequence sets the emotional register for the entire app. The child's very first experience of MindfulNest is darkness resolving into warmth, silence resolving into story. Camera begins tight on the knotwork, pulls back as the book opens. The sound of the cover opening should be deep and resonant --- old leather, old wood --- with a whisper of chimes underneath as the first page catches light.*

# PAGE 1 --- \"Long Ago\"

**Duration:** \~7 seconds

**Transition:** Page turn (parchment rustling)

## Visual

A vast, ancient forest seen from within --- the viewer is standing on a path of golden-brown earth, looking into the trees. The forest is impossibly old. The trees are enormous --- trunks wider than houses, bark covered in moss and glowing lichen, roots that break through the ground like the backs of sleeping animals. Golden light filters through the canopy in visible shafts, each beam alive with drifting pollen and tiny floating motes of light. The air itself seems to shimmer.

The path ahead curves gently into shadow, but it is not a threatening darkness --- it is mystery, invitation. Along the edges of the path, wildflowers grow in unnatural abundance --- blues, purples, golds --- their petals faintly luminous, as though lit from within.

Celtic decorative borders frame the page --- delicate knotwork in gold and deep green that echoes the interlacing roots below.

**Parallax layers:** Background (deep forest, light shafts) drifts slowly left. Midground (tree trunks, path) stays relatively stable. Foreground (wildflowers, floating motes, one curling fern frond) drifts slowly right. Motes drift upward throughout.

**Animation:** Leaves drift lazily from above. Light shafts pulse gently, brightening and dimming like breath. A single butterfly crosses the midground. Floating motes rise and swirl.

## Narration

> *Narrator: \"Long ago, in the heart of a great forest, there was a place where magic was real.\"*

**Audio:** Music box melody begins --- simple, high, delicate. One instrument only. Underneath: ambient forest. Distant birdsong. Wind through high branches.

# PAGE 2 --- \"Everdale\"

**Duration:** \~9 seconds

**Transition:** Gentle page turn. A wash of golden warmth floods the frame.

## Visual

Everdale at the absolute height of its glory --- the most beautiful, magical, safe place a child has ever seen.

Wide establishing shot from a gentle hillside, looking down into a valley city built among ancient trees. The dominant structure: a tree-like statue, its exposed heartwood silver-gold with age. Surrounded by a wreath of gorgeous sutbly-moving roses. Cradled in its highest broken branches sits a nest-shaped structure of interwoven petrified branches and carved stone --- the MindfulNest --- blazing with warm amber-gold light that radiates outward like a second sun. Six stones set into the structure glow in distinct colors: blue, yellow, green, red, purple, orange --- vivid, pulsing, alive.

Stone archways line the paths below, each carved with runes that glow in those same six colors. Creatures of all kinds --- bears, foxes, owls, turtles, rabbits, fireflies, deer --- walk together on lantern-lit pathways. They wear simple clothes --- scarves, vests, satchels. Some are laughing. One bear has its arm around a much smaller rabbit. An owl carries a stack of books while chatting with a fox.

Multicolor, glorious scene. Glowing flowers in every color line every path. Waterfalls cascade down mossy stone walls into crystal pools that catch and scatter the MindfulNest's light into rainbows. Lanterns hang from branches --- not flame-lanterns; they glow with the same warm magic that pulses from the central nest. The air is thick with drifting golden particles --- visible magic, like warm snow falling upward.

*A child watching this should feel one thing: I want to go there.*

**Parallax/Animation:** MindfulNest pulses with light like a heartbeat. Six runestones pulse in sequence. Waterfalls sparkle. Creatures walk in small loops. Golden particles rise. Lanterns sway.

## Narration

> *Narrator: \"Everdale was the center of all the magic in the world. And the First Keepers, the secret order of Rune Protectors, kept the MindfulNest safe from all enemies.*
>
> *Their solemn duty was to protect the world's magic.*

**Audio:** A second instrument joins --- warm strings (cello or viola). The melody expands. Underneath: distant waterfall, laughter on wind, the hum of something ancient and alive.

# PAGE 3 --- \"The Great Storm\"

**Duration:** \~10 seconds

**Transition:** Page turn feels heavier, slower. Golden warmth drains from the frame.

## Visual

Same vantage as Page 2 --- but the sky is black. Bruise-purple and green-grey clouds churn. Wind tears through trees. Branches whip sideways, lanterns swing violently, some shattering.

Lightning cracks across the sky --- a branching, sustained bolt that holds for a beat, illuminating everything in cold blue-white. Creatures running. Pathways cracking. Waterfalls churning muddy.

The runestones flicker. One goes dark. Then another. They die in rapid succession: blue --- gone. Yellow --- gone. Green, red, purple --- gone. The last one, orange, holds for one agonizing extra beat... then dies. The MindfulNest goes dark. The golden light contracts inward, shrinking, dimming, until the entire valley is swallowed in shadow.

**The dragon shadow:** At the peak of the storm, a massive shadow passes overhead. Enormous. Vast wings that blot out even the lightning. The shadow crosses left to right, accompanied by a high, distant cry like an eagle's scream distorted through miles of storm wind. There and gone. We never see the creature itself. Only its shadow, only its cry, only the way the wind doubles in its wake.

## Narration

> *Narrator: \"Then one day, a Great Storm came. Everdale was destroyed in a day. The Keepers were scattered. And the Runestones went dark.\"*

*\[Beat. Lightning. Thunder. Wind. Runestones begin dying.\]*

*\[Dragon shadow crosses. Eagle-scream cry. Last runestone dies.\]*

> *\"And when it was over... Everdale was silent.\"*

**Audio:** Warm ensemble cuts instantly --- replaced by low rumbling drones. Deep irregular percussion. The music box melody returns distorted, slowed, minor key. At "Everdale was silent" --- everything stops. Dead silence for 1.5 seconds.

# PAGE 5 --- \"Everdale Waited\"

**Duration:** \~7 seconds

**Transition:** Soft, muted page turn. Parchment looks older, as if centuries passed.

## Visual

Everdale in ruin. Same valley, same composition as Pages 2 and 4 --- transformed by time and abandonment.

The great tree's trunk is ash-grey, weathered, cracked. The MindfulNest sits dark and tangled with thick vines. The six runestones are reduced to ordinary gray egg-shaped rocks.

Stone archways still stand but runes are dark. Grey-green moss of neglect covers everything. Paths cracked, overgrown. Waterfalls gone --- dry stone channels stained with mineral deposits.

No creatures. No lanterns. No golden light. The only color: the faintest pulse of green-grey light deep inside the MindfulNest --- like the last ember in a fire that went out a hundred years ago.

*It is still beautiful. Hauntingly so. The ruins have a dignity. You can see what this was. And that makes what it IS all the more heartbreaking.*

**Animation:** Almost nothing moves. That is the horror of it. One vine sways. One leaf drifts. The faint green-grey pulse inside the MindfulNest beats once, twice --- barely visible.

## Narration

> *Narrator: \" The creatures ran away. The Order of the First Keepers scattered. And Everdale waited, alone.\"*

**Audio:** Single sustained cello note --- low, mournful, held. No melody. Wind through empty stone corridors. A distant creak.

# PAGE 6 --- \"None Could\"

**Duration:** \~6 seconds

## Visual

The overgrown entrance to Everdale --- a stone archway nearly swallowed by roots and vines. Through the arch, only darkness.

A progression of silhouettes before the entrance --- figures across centuries, left to right. A tall figure with a staff stops, reaches toward the arch, drops its hand, turns away. A smaller figure carrying a lantern --- the lantern dies as it approaches. A pair of figures get the closest, reaching the threshold --- but something pushes them back. Each silhouette more faded than the last.

## Narration

> *Narrator: \"The legend of Everdale was told across all the Kingdoms of the world. Over the centuries, many adventurers tried to find the lost City of Magic. Tried to awaken it. None have succeeded.*

**Audio:** Sustained cello continues. Silence between words is the music. Wind. A distant owl.

# FINAL PAGE --- \"It Felt Something\"

**Duration:** \~8 seconds

**Transition:** Slow, deliberate page turn. Camera begins a slow push-in that continues through the entire page and does not stop.

## Visual

Close-up of the mossy stone entrance. The stone arch fills most of the frame. Carved into the keystone, beneath centuries of moss and lichen: the word EVERDALE. Below, a faded wooden signpost leans at an angle. Ground carpeted in moss, dead leaves, tiny mushrooms.

Then --- deep beneath the moss --- a pulse. A faint golden glow, the size of a fist, blooms for one heartbeat beneath the ground and fades. The moss trembles slightly.

Three seconds of stillness.

The pulse comes again --- stronger. Warmer. Golden light seeps through cracks between stones, threads of gold reaching outward like roots. The moss glows from underneath. The mushrooms catch the light.

The camera pushes in --- through the arch, into the darkness beyond --- and just before the screen goes dark, one final pulse of gold, deep inside Everdale, beating like a heart that has decided to start again.

## Narration

> *Narrator: \"And then one day...\"*

*\[First pulse of gold beneath the moss.\]*

> *\"... a child set foot on in the Lost City.*

*\[Second pulse --- stronger, warmer. Golden light spreads. Camera pushes in. Screen goes warm-dark.\]*

**Audio:** Music box melody from Page 1 returns on a single harp string, slow, tender. On the second pulse: a low warm chord blooms --- not resolving, but opening. The feeling: something is about to begin. Chord holds as screen fades to warm darkness.

*This is the handoff to Scene 2 (Avatar Selection). The warm darkness becomes the loading state. The child's first action should feel like an answer to the question the storybook just asked: the magic felt something. That something was you.*

# CRITICAL PRODUCTION NOTES

**Emotional arc:** Beauty → Awe → Desire → Loss → Grief → Hopelessness → Hope. The entire sequence is designed so that by the Final Page, the child NEEDS Everdale to come back.

**What the child should feel at the end:** "That place was amazing. Something terrible happened to it. Nobody could fix it. But now something is waking up --- and I think it might be because of me."

**The narrator:** NOT the Guide Bird. NOT a character. The narrator is the book itself --- or perhaps the person who wrote it long ago. Timeless, genderless warmth, like a story told around a fire by someone who was there.

**Dragon shadow (Page 4):** Never named, never explained, never shown clearly. A shadow and a cry --- a seed for the larger narrative. Children will ask "what was that?" The answer comes arcs later.

**The golden pulse (Final Page):** The most important visual in the entire sequence. Must feel alive --- like a heartbeat. The warmth of the gold must contrast sharply with the grey-green desolation. It is the first moment of color since Page 3. The child should gasp.

**Voice direction:** Record storm pages (4--5) and hopeful ending (Final Page) as separate emotional takes. Voice should break slightly --- not tears, but weight --- on "Everdale was silent." Lighten with quiet wonder on "it felt something." The pause before "something" is sacred.
