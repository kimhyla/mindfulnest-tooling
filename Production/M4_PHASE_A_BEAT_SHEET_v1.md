**Creature:** Ember the Fox
**Domain:** Kindness / Compassion
**Spell:** Heart-Sending Spell

## What Phase B Needs the Child to Have Seen

Before the child closes their eyes and hears "Ahh... you've come to send your Kindness magic," they must have:

1. **Seen kind thoughts travel visibly from sender to receiver** — so "think of something good for them... and send it" makes sense
2. **Seen the receiver respond** (Sweetrose grows/brightens) — so "send it to them" has a concrete target
3. **Seen the sender get brighter/warmer too** — so "check inside... are you feeling a little warmer and nicer too?" lands as recognition, not surprise
4. **Heard Guide Bird name the double-up** — so "That's the double-up" in Phase B is vocabulary they already own
5. **Understood that the Sweetrose needs warmth and love** — so the target choice in Phase B ("the Sweetrose, or someone from your life") is grounded

## SECTION 1: NARRATIVE SETUP (from skeleton — not technique)

These beats are story. They bring Ember on stage and set up WHY the spell is needed. The child watches but doesn't practice anything yet.

---

**Beat 1 — Ember Arrives**
- SCREEN: Ember the fox walks toward the MindfulNest from the forest edge. She looks curious, tentative.
- VOICE: Ember: "Hello there..."
- ACTION: Child watches.
- PURPOSE: Introduces Ember. She's not in distress — she's drawn by curiosity.

**Beat 2 — Introductions**
- SCREEN: Tessa, Luna, and Ember talking at the MindfulNest.
- VOICE: Tessa: "Hi. ... Can we help you?" / Ember explains she saw beautiful lights, asks what the runestones are. / Luna: "Runestones." / Tessa: "Magical ones." / Luna explains they're waking the stones to restore Everdale.
- ACTION: Child watches.
- PURPOSE: Establishes Ember as newcomer. Recaps the quest briefly for children returning after a break.

**Beat 3 — The Inscription Puzzle**
- SCREEN: Luna studying the third inscription on the MindfulNest.
- VOICE: Luna recaps the first two stones and their inscriptions, then: "This one says 'Let the flowers bloom,' but I have no idea how we're supposed to do that."
- ACTION: Child watches.
- PURPOSE: Sets up the problem the spell will solve. The inscription is the riddle; the technique is the answer.

**Beat 4 — Ember's Idea**
- SCREEN: Ember thinks, then pulls a tiny Sweetrose sprout from her pocket. It unfurls slightly in the sunlight.
- VOICE: Ember: "Hmmm.... Oh! I have an idea!" / "It's from Foxhollow. It's a Sweetrose plant. I wonder if it would take to these roots."
- ACTION: Child watches.
- PURPOSE: Ember arrives with the solution without knowing it. She's not in distress; she's contributing. Models that kindness is something you already carry with you.

**Beat 5 — Guide Bird Sets Up the Spell**
- SCREEN: Guide Bird addresses the camera. The Sweetrose sprout sits on the MindfulNest, small and waiting.
- VOICE: Guide Bird: "Ember's got a perfect idea. Sweetroses are beautiful flowers. But they're fussy about where they're willing to be planted. They only like warm, soft places with lots of love and sunlight. Let's cast a Magic Spell on the MindfulNest, and make a perfect spot for the Sweetrose sprout to root into. It's called the Heart-Sending Spell. Ready?"
- ACTION: Child watches (or taps to confirm "Ready").
- PURPOSE: Names the spell. Establishes that the Sweetrose needs warmth and love — the child's kind thoughts ARE that warmth. Transitions from story to technique demo.

## SECTION 2: PHASE A DEMO (technique demonstration — Guide Bird narrates)

Guide Bird narrates the interactive demo. The child taps to create and send a kind thought, sees the double-up, Guide Bird names it, then Myrrhin's voice takes over for Phase B.

---

**Beat 6 — The Child Sends (Interactive, Guide Bird narrating)**
- SCREEN: The Sweetrose sprout on the MindfulNest, small and waiting. A tap prompt appears.
- VOICE: Guide Bird: "Let's send the Sweetrose a kind thought. Tap to make one."
- ACTION: **Child taps (tap 1).** A glowing thought bubble appears with text: "Grow big and strong!" Guide Bird: "Now tap to send it." **Child taps (tap 2).** The bubble lifts off, travels to the Sweetrose, and lands. The Sweetrose brightens — and the child's avatar brightens at the same time.
- PURPOSE: Two taps, two actions: create the thought, send the thought. No creative work from the child — the thought is provided. On the send, both Sweetrose and child's avatar glow simultaneously. The double-up is seen, not yet named.

**Beat 7 — Guide Bird Names the Double-Up**
- SCREEN: Both the Sweetrose and the child's avatar still glowing. Guide Bird addressing camera.
- VOICE: Guide Bird: "See? Kind thoughts have a double-up magic power. They help whoever gets them AND whoever sends them."
- ACTION: Child watches.
- PURPOSE: Names the double-up simply. Two sentences. The child owns "double-up," "kind thoughts," and "Kindness magic" before Phase B.

**Beat 8 — Transition to Phase B (Myrrhin takes over)**
- SCREEN: Gentle fade. The Sweetrose and MindfulNest settle into a warm ambient glow. Screen softens.
- VOICE: Guide Bird: "Now close your eyes. Listen to the voice on the wind..." Fade to Myrrhin's voice beginning Phase B: "Ahh... you've come to send your Kindness magic. Good."
- ACTION: Child closes eyes. Phase B begins (narrated by Myrrhin, not Guide Bird).
- PURPOSE: Clean handoff from Guide Bird (interactive, energetic) to Myrrhin (meditative, warm). The voice change signals the shift from watching to feeling.

## Phase A → Phase B Vocabulary Bridge

| Phase A establishes | Phase B uses |
|---|---|
| "Think of something good for the Sweetrose" (Beat 6) | "Think of something good for them. Something that would make them really happy." (INSTRUCTION) |
| Kind thought travels as glowing bubble (Beat 6, tap 2) | "You saw the kind words travel as glowing bubbles" (CONNECTION) |
| Sweetrose brightens when kind thought lands (Beat 6, tap 2) | "the Sweetrose... or someone from your life you care about" (SETUP) |
| Both Sweetrose and child's avatar glow (Beat 6, tap 2) | "Are you feeling a little warmer and nicer too?" (DEEPENING) |
| "Double-up magic power" (Beat 7) | "That's the double-up" (DEEPENING) |
| "They help whoever gets them AND whoever sends them" (Beat 7) | "Your kind thoughts go out to the other person... and they also make you feel nicer and warmer too" (DEEPENING) |
| Guide Bird → Myrrhin voice transition (Beat 8) | Myrrhin narrates all of Phase B (warm, meditative voice) |

## Design Notes

1. **Guide Bird narrates the demo; the child does the action.** No separate "watch Guide Bird do it" step. Guide Bird's voice guides the child through tapping to send — the child is the sender from the start.

2. **Two taps, no creative work.** Tap 1 creates the kind thought (provided automatically: "Grow big and strong!"). Tap 2 sends it. The double-up (both Sweetrose and child's avatar brightening) happens on the send. The child's cognitive work comes later in Phase B — Phase A is purely instructional.

3. **The double-up naming is two sentences.** "Kind thoughts have a double-up magic power. They help whoever gets them AND whoever sends them." Simple, complete, done. No elaboration needed — the child just SAW it happen.

4. **Voice handoff: Guide Bird → Myrrhin.** Guide Bird is energetic and interactive (Phase A). Myrrhin is warm and meditative (Phase B). The voice change IS the transition from watching/doing to feeling. This is consistent with the voice architecture: Myrrhin narrates all Phase B meditations.

5. **No breathing instruction in Phase A.** The breath rhythm is Phase B's contribution. Phase A only shows WHAT to send and WHY it works — not HOW to send it with breath.

6. **Ember is not the recipient of the kind thoughts.** The Sweetrose is. Ember provided the sprout; the child provides the warmth. This keeps Ember as a contributor, not someone who needs help.

7. **"Kind thoughts" not "kind words."** The child THINKS something good, they don't necessarily speak words. Matches the approved Phase B language.
