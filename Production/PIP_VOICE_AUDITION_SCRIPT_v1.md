# PIP VOICE AUDITION SCRIPT

**Purpose:** Paste this into ElevenLabs playground for each candidate voice. Covers all emotional registers Pip uses across the app. Listen for: warmth, energy, personality, range, and clear distinction from Myrrdin.

**Total length:** ~35 seconds spoken at Pip's natural pace.

---

## THE SCRIPT

Psst! Hey! Something's wrong at the pond — Tessa's shell is shaking and she won't come out. I think she's really scared.

You know that feeling when everything gets loud and spinny and you just want to hide? Tessa gets that too. But I know a spell that can help. Want me to show you how it works?

Whoa — look at Tessa's shell — it's starting to shimmer! You did that!

Between you and me, Bramble tried to plant a sandwich yesterday. It did not go well.

Dr. Sarah says: I'm so proud of how many spells you've learned. Keep it up, you're doing amazing!

Hey, welcome back. Ember's been waiting for you. She kept looking toward the path all morning.

I know it looks bad. I know. But you rebuilt this place once. Remember that. And I'm not going anywhere. Just so you know.

---

## WHAT EACH SECTION TESTS

| Lines | Register | What to Listen For |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Urgency + excitement** (Call dialogue) | Does the voice convey real concern without sounding panicked? Does "psst" sound natural, not forced? |
| 2 | **Warmth + empathy** (Buy-In dialogue) | Can it slow down and be gentle? Does "You know that feeling" sound like a friend, not a teacher? |
| 3 | **Wonder + celebration** (Tier transition / Win) | Does excitement sound genuine? Does "You did that!" land with pride? |
| 4 | **Humor** (Talk to Pip — Funny category) | Does the comedic timing work? Does it sound like someone who thinks they're funny? |
| 5 | **Message delivery** (Therapist message via Pip) | Can it frame someone else's words naturally? Does the shift between Pip's intro and the quoted message feel smooth? |
| 6 | **Quiet warmth** (Return-to-app hook) | Can it be soft without being sleepy? Does "kept looking toward the path" feel lived-in? |
| 7 | **Emotional depth** (Crisis moment — Arc 2) | This is the hardest test. Can this voice carry real weight? Does "I'm not going anywhere" land without being melodramatic? |

---

## EVALUATION CRITERIA

Score each candidate 1–5 on:

| Criterion | What It Means | Myrrdin Comparison |
|---|---|---|
| **Friend energy** | Sounds like a companion, not an authority | Myrrdin = grandparent. Pip = best friend. |
| **Speed comfort** | Sounds natural at fast-ish delivery (Pip is quick and punchy) | Myrrdin is deliberately slow. Pip should be noticeably faster. |
| **Emotional range** | Can shift from excited → gentle → funny → serious within 30 seconds | Myrrdin stays in one register (calm). Pip needs all registers. |
| **Distinctness from Myrrdin** | A child would never confuse the two voices | If it sounds even slightly grandparent-like, reject. |
| **50-hour test** | Could a child hear this voice for hundreds of sessions without it grating? | Both voices need this. Pip's energy can't be shrill or grating at volume. |
| **The "shimmer" line** | Does "You did that!" make you smile? | This is the gut check. If this line doesn't land, the voice isn't Pip. |

## SETTINGS TO TEST AFTER PICKING A WINNER

**Starting point (adjust from here):**

| Setting | Pip Target | Myrrdin Reference | Why Different |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stability | 50–60% | 65–75% | Pip should sound more dynamic, more varied between lines |
| Clarity + Similarity | 75–85% | 75–85% | Same — want the voice to stay consistent |
| Style Exaggeration | 30–40% | 15–25% | Pip has MORE personality, more expressiveness |

**After dialing settings:** Re-run the full script above. Then run JUST the crisis lines (6 and 7) to make sure the settings don't make serious moments sound cartoonish. The voice needs to hold weight at high style exaggeration.
