# Creature Vocabulary Appendix — MindfulNest Arc 1

**Version:** 1.0 — April 11, 2026
**Status:** Reference Tool — Creature Physical Vocabulary for Narrative Generation
**Purpose:** Establish species-appropriate physical vocabulary for each Arc 1 creature. Prevents Haiku from hallucinating physical capabilities that don't match the creature's species, anatomy, or established behavior patterns in the skeleton.

## M1: TESSA (Turtle, Female)

### Species & Physical Description
- **Species:** Turtle
- **Size:** Small
- **Signature Accessories:** Woven scarf with wave patterns, leather pouch of smooth pebbles
- **Shell Characteristics:** Subtle mosaic patterns; moves slowly, that's okay — "Tessa teaches that slow is strong"
- **Eyes:** Round, gentle

### Movement Vocabulary
- Slow, deliberate movement (canonical: "Tessa doesn't say much, but when she does, it's always worth listening to")
- Dipping toes in pond
- Tucking into shell for a nap then peeking out
- Moving slowly and patiently — slowness is a strength, not a weakness
- Arranging pebbles
- Watching clouds reflected in water

### Emotional Expression Vocabulary
- **Gentle / patient:** Soft voice, measured pace
- **Shy / withdrawn:** Tucking into shell, peeking out cautiously
- **Afraid of loud sudden noises:** No dramatization — just natural recoil
- **Physical pain/discomfort:** Soft crying (not screaming or dramatic), body locked in protective flinching
- **Embarrassment:** Trying to shake off tears, downplaying her own hurt
- **Relief:** A softening, stopping flinching, stillness

### Sound Vocabulary
- **Voice:** Speaks softly, measured pace
- **Characteristic sounds:** Soft sniffling when upset (not loud sobbing)
- **Silence:** Tessa's default state — "Tessa doesn't say much"

### Key Visual Identifiers
- Woven scarf with wave patterns
- Leather pouch of pebbles at her side
- Shell with mosaic patterns
- Painterly character design — expressive, charming, not cartoonish, with rich detail and texture
- Round, gentle eyes
- Small turtle wearing a scarf — she looks dressed and intentional, not wild

### Things Tessa CANNOT Do Physically
- Cannot move quickly or rush (contradicts her nature)
- Cannot climb easily (turtles are not climbers)
- Cannot make loud sounds or shout
- Cannot retract into shell and be completely invisible — peeking out is always part of the action
- Cannot stand on hind legs for extended periods
- Cannot reach high places without assistance
- Cannot jump or leap
- Cannot sprint or move with urgency

## M2: LUNA (Owl, Female)

### Species & Physical Description
- **Species:** Owl
- **Size:** Small to medium
- **Signature Accessories:** Tiny round spectacles, constellation-patterned cloak (slightly too big)
- **Feathers:** Messy-charming, never sleek
- **Eyes:** Enormous, expressive
- **Head:** Can rotate 180° for comedic moments

### Movement Vocabulary
- Quick, eager, slightly breathless speech
- Reading intensely (hunched over scrolls)
- Gasping through telescope
- Reorganizing books then getting distracted
- Head spinning 180° for comedic effect
- Scribbling notes rapidly
- Flapping wings or gesturing wildly when excited
- Ruffling feathers when flustered
- Landing/perching on surfaces (tree branch, shelf, etc.)

### Emotional Expression Vocabulary
- **Curious / excited:** Rapid speech, gesturing wildly, eyes wide, flapping
- **Overwhelmed / scattered:** Racing thoughts manifested as physical restlessness — can't settle, eyes darting
- **Focusing / concentrating:** Head tilting, squinting at objects, using telescope, settling down
- **Flustered:** Ruffling feathers, rapid clicky sounds, adjusting spectacles
- **Discovery moment:** Gasping, spinning around excitedly, gesturing at the object of discovery
- **Confused:** Head tilt, one eye narrowed, consulting maps/notes

### Sound Vocabulary
- **Voice:** Quick, eager, slightly breathless, talks too fast
- **Characteristic sounds:** Gasping, excited chirping, rapid clicky owl sounds
- **Racing thoughts:** Verbal stutter/overlap when trying to express multiple ideas at once
- **Signature vocalization:** Owl hoot when making a realization (e.g., "HOOOOT — that's IT!")

### Key Visual Identifiers
- Tiny round spectacles (signature)
- Constellation-patterned cloak that's slightly too big
- Messy-charming feathers
- Enormous expressive eyes (can convey emotion across the screen)
- Small owl scholar — looks like a tiny scholar-adventurer
- Often holding scrolls, maps, or books

### Things Luna CANNOT Do Physically
- Cannot move slowly or patiently (contradicts her racing-mind nature)
- Cannot be silent or still for long periods
- Cannot have neat, sleek feathers (her messiness is canonical)
- Cannot keep her attention on one thing without visible distraction
- Cannot speak without gesturing or moving
- Cannot have a subdued voice or move quietly
- Cannot focus on task without getting distracted by interesting details
- Cannot write or organize without knocking things over due to distraction

## M3: BENSON (Bunny, Male)

### Species & Physical Description
- **Species:** Bunny/Rabbit
- **Size:** Tiny
- **Signature Accessory:** Small knitted vest with a single button (belonged to a sibling — he holds onto it)
- **Ears:** Enormous, expressive — droop when worried, perk up when brave
- **Eyes:** Big, round, always slightly wide
- **Nose:** Twitches when something feels wrong
- **Build:** Travels light (no satchel, no art supplies)

### Movement Vocabulary
- Peeking nervously from burrow then hopping back in
- Tiny brave hops (practiced hopping when building courage)
- Ears shooting straight up at sudden sounds
- Cautious sniffing of flowers
- Hiccupping when nervous (involuntary physical response)
- Hiding behind larger creatures' wings when startled
- Ducking down, trying to make himself smaller
- Hesitant movements followed by a push to be brave
- Small, careful steps forward
- Nose twitching at danger

### Emotional Expression Vocabulary
- **Fearful / anxious:** Hiccups (involuntary), ears droop, nose twitches, voice gets quiet and shaky
- **Startled:** Ears shoot straight up, immediate jump/recoil, eyes go wide, may dive for burrow
- **Nervous:** Ears partially drooped, sniffling, talking between hiccups and sniffs
- **Worried:** Wringing paws, ears half-down, pacing in small circles
- **Brave:** Ears perk up straight, nose settles, voice steadies, small determined movements
- **Crying / distressed:** Talking between sobs and sniffs, hiccups, "SNIFF SNIFF — HICCUP"
- **Encouraged:** Ears perking up, voice getting a little stronger, first small brave hop

### Sound Vocabulary
- **Voice:** Quiet, slightly nervous, lights up when encouraged
- **Characteristic sounds:** Hiccups (signature involuntary response when nervous), sniffling, soft chirping/squeaking
- **When scared:** Whimpering between hiccups — "... what if... HICCUP ... what if the hawk..."
- **When brave:** Voice gets a little steadier and louder
- **Signature vocalization:** Hiccup followed by a surprised "eep!" — physical and emotional at once

### Key Visual Identifiers
- Enormous expressive ears (primary emotion indicator)
- Tiny size (smallest creature in the party)
- Knitted vest with single button (from sibling)
- Big, round, perpetually slightly-wide eyes
- Twitching nose
- No extra gear — travels light
- You can read his emotional state from across the screen via his ears

### Things Benson CANNOT Do Physically
- Cannot move quickly or run at full speed (he's tiny and anxious)
- Cannot be the first to enter a space without visible hesitation
- Cannot have calm ears or a calm expression (they're always slightly worried)
- Cannot speak without stuttering or pausing when scared
- Cannot suppress his hiccups (they're involuntary)
- Cannot stand tall or make himself appear bigger
- Cannot stop his nose from twitching when danger is near
- Cannot hide his emotions — his body betrays him visibly
- Cannot be silent when scared (sniffling, quiet crying, hiccups are constant)
- Cannot be the brave one without visible effort and fear still present

## M4: EMBER (Fox, Female)

### Species & Physical Description
- **Species:** Fox
- **Size:** Small
- **Signature Accessories:** Pendant on a cord that glows when she's being kind (visual identifier of the Art of Kindness), tiny wildflower (sometimes carries)
- **Fur:** Slightly messy in an endearing way, not pristine
- **Eyes:** Warm amber eyes
- **Ears:** Oversized (fox-proportionate)
- **Physical detail:** Arrived with a broken paw that isn't healing right (Arc 1 setup)

### Movement Vocabulary
- Chasing her own tail then looking embarrassed
- Presenting a flower (to another character or to the child)
- Playful pounce on a leaf
- Curling by fireplace
- Arranging treasures (in her den)
- Bouncing or trotting (playful movement)
- Sidling up to characters (warm, affectionate approach)
- Limping slightly (broken paw canon — though healing progresses)
- Pouncing playfully on imaginary prey
- Playful wrestling with other creatures

### Emotional Expression Vocabulary
- **Warm-hearted / playful:** Energy bouncing, tail wagging, pouncing, presenting gifts
- **Mischievous:** Grin, head tilt, eyes glinting, preparing to do something funny
- **Hurt / sensitive:** Tucking in on herself, dimming energy, ears pulling back slightly
- **Ashamed or embarrassed:** Looking away, quietly saying "I'm so dumb," shoulders dropping
- **Kind / compassionate:** Pendant glows (visual cue), soft eyes, gentle voice, reaching toward another
- **Acting tough:** Bright energy masking deeper hurt — overdoing the playfulness
- **Vulnerable moment:** Moment of stillness, soft eyes, letting guard down

### Sound Vocabulary
- **Voice:** Bright, energetic, with occasional soft moments
- **Characteristic sounds:** Fox yips, chirps, playful growls
- **When emotional:** Voice drops to a whisper or becomes very quiet
- **Signature vocalization:** Playful yip or chirp when doing something mischievous
- **When kind:** Voice becomes noticeably softer and warmer

### Key Visual Identifiers
- Glowing pendant on a cord (signature — connects to Art of Kindness visually)
- Small, scruffy fox with oversized ears
- Warm amber eyes
- Slightly messy fur in an endearing way
- Sometimes carries a tiny wildflower
- Physical contrast: acts tough but is deeply sensitive

### Things Ember CANNOT Do Physically
- Cannot be completely still or quiet (she's a ball of energy)
- Cannot hide her emotions for long (actions betray her feelings)
- Cannot move without some playfulness or energy
- Cannot speak in a way that sounds uncaring or cold
- Cannot have her pendant NOT glow when she's being genuinely kind (it's a visual rule)
- Cannot be aggressive or harsh (even acting tough, there's warmth underneath)
- Cannot move with confidence if she's being hurt — limping or pulling back is canonical
- Cannot ignore an opportunity to be kind or make someone laugh
- Cannot appear pristine or perfectly groomed (her endearing scruffle is canonical)

## M5: BORK (Firefly, Male)

### Species & Physical Description
- **Species:** Firefly / Anthroborkian X97
- **Size:** Tiny
- **Signature Accessories:** Silk cape (slightly too big), tiny glowing loudspeaker
- **Light:** Flickering unpredictably — brighter when happy, dimmer when sad, cuts out entirely when exhausted
- **Movement:** Drifts more than walks, can hover
- **Voice output:** Rapid clicky insect language sounds like a "broken music box" when flustered

### Movement Vocabulary
- Drifting (floating flight, not walking)
- Landing on flowers or leaves
- Glow pulsing like breathing (visual rhythm)
- Hovering in place
- Sputtering glow back on after it cuts out (looking around to see if anyone noticed)
- Flying unsteadily when exhausted
- Adjusting invisible tie (formal gesture, oddly endearing)
- Practicing formal introductions to no one
- Taking flight suddenly when agitated
- Settling down slowly when given permission to rest

### Emotional Expression Vocabulary
- **Pompous / formal:** Stiff posture (as much as a tiny firefly has), speaking formally, clipped tone
- **Exhausted / burned out:** Glow flickering and dimming, flying less steadily, voice becoming quieter and slower
- **Flustered / overwhelmed:** Rapid clicky insect language, glow flickering erratically, speaking faster
- **Getting warm / relaxing:** Glow steadying and brightening, cape shimmering more, voice becoming slightly less formal
- **Proud / dignified:** Glow shining bright, cape shimmering, chest puffed out (as much as possible), formal speech even more pronounced
- **Vulnerable / lonely:** Glow cutting out, small voice, speaking about his mission's weight
- **Amused / dry humor:** Glow steady and bright, a pause before dry observation, barely-suppressed amusement in voice

### Sound Vocabulary
- **Voice:** Formal, clipped, slightly exasperated at informality around him
- **Characteristic sounds:** Rapid clicky insect language (like broken music box) when flustered, formal "ahem" before announcements
- **Signature vocalization:** "WE DO NOT PLAY, BOBBLE-HEADED CREATURE!!!" — loudspeaker-amplified, indignant
- **Formal speech:** Uses big words, long sentence structures, diplomatic phrasing
- **At rest:** Voice becomes slightly softer, less clipped, occasionally dry wit
- **Exhaustion:** Voice drops to a whisper or becomes monotone, loses the formal cadence

### Key Visual Identifiers
- Glowing loudspeaker (tiny, comically small, official communication device from Swarm)
- Silk cape that's slightly too big and shimmers when moving
- Flickering glow that responds to emotional state
- Tiny firefly with enormous presence and dignity
- Appears as a tiny warm light in darkness, except when glow cuts out

### Things Bork CANNOT Do Physically
- Cannot move quickly on the ground (fireflies don't walk fast)
- Cannot make sound without the loudspeaker (his tiny voice wouldn't carry)
- Cannot have a glow that doesn't respond to his emotional state
- Cannot stay awake indefinitely (exhaustion causes glow to cut out completely)
- Cannot move with less-than-formal posture (even drifting, he maintains dignity)
- Cannot speak informally for extended periods (formality is core to his character)
- Cannot be invisible or unnoticed (his glow makes him visible, and he's OK with that)
- Cannot move heavily or with impact (he's a tiny flying creature)
- Cannot survive without resting eventually (the mission is wearing him out)
- Cannot let go of his formal designation (Anthroborkian X97 matters to him deeply)

## M6: BRAMBLE (Bear, Male)

### Species & Physical Description
- **Species:** Bear
- **Size:** Big, larger than other creatures
- **Signature Accessories:** Leather tool belt for garden work with rune-carved tools (slightly too big for the belt to look perfectly fitted)
- **Build:** Burly, Baloo-style papa energy, big but clumsy and gentle
- **Paws:** Enormous, gentle
- **Physical trait:** Gets startled easily despite his size
- **Movement style:** Often in a wide grounded stance when excited or trying to be still

### Movement Vocabulary
- Sniffing a flower blissfully
- Stretching hugely (full-body bear stretch)
- Touching tree bark gently with enormous paws
- Sitting still watching a butterfly
- Tending garden with enormous, gentle paws
- Settling into a wide grounded stance (signature grounding technique)
- Wiggles and restless movement when excited or overstimulated (physical energy in body)
- Planting feet firmly and pushing (grounding power)
- Lumbering onto a scene (slow but large movement)
- Knocking things over accidentally despite gentleness (size-to-care mismatch)

### Emotional Expression Vocabulary
- **Excited / energized:** Physical energy becomes visible restless movement, wiggles, dancing, pacing
- **Overwhelmed / overstimulated:** Gets restless, tries to settle, needs to ground through wide stance
- **Calm / grounded / planted:** Settles into wide stance, stillness comes, focus appears
- **Joyful:** Dancing, large movements, enthusiastic gestures, loud but warm voice
- **Gentle / careful:** Movements slow and deliberate, paws are extremely gentle despite size
- **Startled:** Despite size, recoils, flinches, then settles
- **Protective:** Big body positioning between threat and smaller creature, eyes focused and alert
- **Noticing details:** Head tilting, leaning in close, gentle touching with one paw

### Sound Vocabulary
- **Voice:** Deep, warm, enthusiastic
- **Volume:** Can be loud when excited (matches his size and energy)
- **Characteristic sounds:** Bear huffing/snuffling sounds, contented rumbling when calm, excited growls
- **Signature behavior:** Speaks enthusiastically about nature, plants, his cubs
- **When grounded:** Voice becomes calmer and more measured
- **When overwhelmed:** Voice may rise in pitch, becomes agitated, more huffing sounds

### Key Visual Identifiers
- Leather tool belt with rune-carved garden tools (signature)
- Big, burly bear with Baloo-style papa energy
- Enormous gentle paws (physical contradiction: size vs. gentleness)
- Gets startled easily despite his size (unexpected for a large creature)
- Settles into wide grounded stance (visual trademark of his grounding)
- Gentle-giant contrast — big body, soft heart

### Things Bramble CANNOT Do Physically
- Cannot move quietly or unnoticed (he's large and his movements have impact)
- Cannot be in a tight, enclosed space without feeling overwhelmed
- Cannot suppress his enthusiastic energy without actively grounding
- Cannot touch delicate things without being extremely careful (his size matters)
- Cannot move without some physical energy — he's an active, embodied creature
- Cannot keep from noticing small details (his attention to nature is canonical)
- Cannot speak in a way that sounds uncaring or clinical
- Cannot stay in a small space without that showing physically (overwhelm becomes visible)
- Cannot be the fastest or most agile creature (size vs. agility)
- Cannot ignore the wellbeing of smaller creatures (protective instinct is core)

## CROSS-CREATURE ANTI-HALLUCINATION RULES

### Rule: No Creature Can Borrow Another Creature's Physical Capabilities

| Turtle | Owl | Bunny | Fox | Firefly | Bear |
|--------|-----|-------|-----|---------|------|
| Slow, deliberate | Quick, eager | Tiny, anxious | Playful, bouncy | Drifts/hovers | Large, strong |
| Cannot rush | Cannot be still | Cannot be tall | Cannot be quiet | Cannot walk steadily | Cannot be quiet |
| Cannot climb | Cannot slow down | Cannot jump high | Cannot be pristine | Cannot move fast | Cannot fit small spaces |
| Cannot shout | Cannot be silent | Cannot be brave easily | Cannot be cold | Cannot stay awake indefinitely | Cannot move quietly |

### Rule: Emotional Expression Must Be Species-Appropriate

**Never:** Have a character express emotion in a way that contradicts their species physiology.

**Examples of violations to AVOID:**
- Tessa shaking with anger or moving frantically (turtles are slow; Tessa is gentle)
- Luna sitting still and quietly for extended periods (owls have racing minds; Luna is scattered)
- Benson not showing visible fear signs (hiccups, ear droop, nose twitch are involuntary)
- Ember suppressing playfulness or energy (warmth and playfulness are core)
- Bork flying without flickering glow responding to his emotions (glow is his emotional meter)
- Bramble moving quietly or delicately as his default (he's big; size has impact)

### Rule: Voice and Sound Must Match Species

**Tessa:** Soft, measured, sparse
**Luna:** Quick, breathless, with owl hoots and excited chirps
**Benson:** Quiet and nervous with hiccups and sniffling
**Ember:** Bright and energetic with fox yips and chirps
**Bork:** Formal and clipped with clicky insect language when flustered
**Bramble:** Deep, warm, enthusiastic, with bear huffs and rumbles

### Rule: Signature Accessories Are Not Optional

These are canonical visual identifiers that appear regularly:
- **Tessa:** Woven scarf with wave patterns (always)
- **Luna:** Tiny round spectacles + constellation cloak (always)
- **Benson:** Knitted vest with single button from sibling (always)
- **Ember:** Glowing pendant on cord (always; glows when kind)
- **Bork:** Silk cape + tiny glowing loudspeaker (always)
- **Bramble:** Leather tool belt with rune-carved tools (always)

## USING THIS APPENDIX WITH HAIKU

When Haiku generates narrative text for a creature:

1. **Check the creature's physical vocabulary section.** Does the generated action appear in their canonical vocabulary?
2. **Check the anti-hallucination rules.** Does the action violate any species-appropriate boundaries?
3. **Check signature accessories.** Are they mentioned if the creature is in a formal or notable moment?
4. **Check emotional expression.** Is the emotion expressed in a way that matches their physiology?
5. **Check voice/sound.** Does dialogue sound like the creature's established voice?

**If any element fails these checks, regenerate rather than accepting the hallucination.**

Example flag:
- **Generated:** "Tessa raced quickly across the pond, shouting with excitement."
- **Flag:** Turtles don't race. Tessa doesn't shout. Her slowness is a strength, not something to overcome.
- **Corrected:** "Tessa moved slowly across the pond, a small smile crossing her face."
